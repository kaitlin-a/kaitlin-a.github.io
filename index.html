About

I am a computer science and physics student focused on computational robotics, reinforcement learning, and human–robot systems. My research spans adaptive human–AMR warehouse coordination, autonomous-vehicle sensor processing, and engineering systems for disaster response. I am particularly interested in designing intelligent systems that connect algorithmic decision-making with reliable physical operation.

Research & Impact

Reinforcement Learning for Human–AMR Warehouse Coordination — IRES Research Intern, Universität Passau, Germany

Developed a reinforcement-learning framework and data-grounded discrete-event simulation for adaptive service-mode selection in heterogeneous human–AMR warehouse order picking.

  • Manuscript currently under review.
  • Research code maintained privately pending publication.

ROSbag Filtration (RBF) Tool — Robotics Research Intern, Texas A&M University

Autonomous vehicle sensor refinement system using Kalman filtering and signal-to-noise ratio analysis. Developed to remove static or low-quality data segments from ROS bag files, improving downstream modeling and state estimation reliability.

  • View Github Repository: RosBag Filtration (RBF) Node Data Analysis Tool
  • Research Paper: Data Refining: ROSbag Filtration (RBF) Tool

Rescue Operations Assistant (ROA)

Led development of an engineering solution for disaster-response operations, earning first place nationally in the National Science Foundation AACIC Competition. Presented the award-winning project at a national symposium in the Rayburn House Office Building on Capitol Hill in Washington, D.C.
